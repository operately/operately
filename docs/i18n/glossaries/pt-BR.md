# Brazilian Portuguese glossary

Locale: `pt-BR` (Gettext: `pt_BR`). The terms below preserve the native-speaker-reviewed glossary and are the authoritative terminology for Portuguese product copy.

Use the listed forms consistently. Lowercase terms may be capitalized at the start of a sentence or label; preserve the listed spelling and casing of named UI sections. Any terminology changes require native-speaker review before use across the product. See the [internationalization guide](../../internationalization.md).

| English | Meaning/context | Português (Brasil) | Capitalization and usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| company | Organization using Operately | empresa | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| member | Person belonging to a company | membro | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| manager | Person who manages another person | gestor | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| peers | People with the same manager | pares | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| reports (people) | People managed by someone; not documents or reports | subordinados | People only; do not use for report documents. |
| email / emails | Electronic mail; singular and plural | e-mail / e-mails | Keep the hyphen in both forms. |
| space | Shared area within a company | espaço | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| project | Project being delivered | projeto | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| goal | Outcome being pursued | objetivo | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| parent goal | Goal above another goal in the hierarchy | objetivo superior | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| task | Unit of work | tarefa | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| milestone | Project milestone | marco | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| check-in | Progress update | check-in | Keep the hyphen. |
| acknowledge | Action confirming an update has been read | confirmar leitura | Confirms reading, not approval. Use “confirmar a leitura” within sentences. |
| acknowledgement | Confirmation that an update has been read | confirmação de leitura | Use “leitura confirmada” for the completed state. |
| champion | Person accountable for a goal or project | champion | Retain the approved borrowed term. |
| home | Home navigation destination | início | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| my work | Personal work navigation destination | meu trabalho | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| review | Review navigation or review work | revisão | Capitalize when required by the sentence or label. |
| due date | Planned completion date; not the actual completion timestamp | data de conclusão | Use this term consistently for due-date copy. |
| Docs & Files | Documents and files tool name | Docs & Arquivos | Preserve the named tool’s capitalization and ampersand. |
| Comments & Activity | Comments and activity section name | Comentários & Atividade | Preserve the section’s capitalization and ampersand. |
| template | Reusable project template | Template | Preserve the approved capital T in labels. |
